HOW WE BRING GLOBAL LUXURY HERITAGE TO CAIRO

Every headscarf in the LAYL collection is hand-selected from flagship salons in Paris, Milan, and London. We believe luxury in Egypt should never come with doubt, delay, or customs uncertainty.

01

100% Provenance Verification

Every piece is inspected for authentic hallmark signatures, hand-rolled hem tension, and correct silk twill weight before entering our Cairo inventory.

02

Climate-Regulated Care & Storage

Preserved in acid-free archival tissue within temperature and humidity-controlled storage in Cairo to protect delicate silk proteins and cashmere fibers.

03

Private White-Glove Courier

Delivered directly to your residence across Zamalek, New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed, and all Egyptian governorates with discretion and physical authentication certs.
